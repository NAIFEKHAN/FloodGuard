SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY & ETHICAL GOVERNANCE

Governance Principles & Methodological Guardrails

STRICT ADHERENCE
01
Zero Data Fabrication

All inputs originate from authentic portals (IMD, GSI, SOI, DDMP). Unlabeled locations are strictly preserved without synthetic negative labels or imputation.

02
40 Validated Village Universe

Modelling is strictly restricted to 40 validated LGD polygons with exact spatial containment (25 positive, 15 unlabeled). Unmatched areas are not interpolated.

03
Spatial Group Cross-Validation

Leave-One-Taluk-Out (LOTO) 6-fold spatial validation ensures zero spatial leakage across taluk boundaries (LOTO ROC-AUC 0.8747, PR-AUC 0.9426).

04
Non-Destructive Scenario Engine

Rainfall scenarios evaluate in-memory feature perturbations while keeping raw data and SRTM terrain constant. Outputs are demonstration simulations, not live warnings.
